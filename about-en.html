Our reason for being

We exist for
one reason:
to make African mines
more productive.

MINEFECT is a company specialized in integrated solutions for the mining sector. We combine training, equipment supply and engineering to support the sustainable performance of mining operators across Africa.

Because Africa holds some of the world's most significant mineral resources — and the mines that exploit them deserve partners who match their ambitions, understand their constraints, and know their terrain.

01

01 — Training

Professional Training

Accidents and productivity losses often stem from inadequate training. MINEFECT provides targeted programmes to strengthen technical and operational skills on site — securing day-to-day operations across every level of the workforce.

From equipment operation to preventive maintenance and HSE management — our training programmes are designed for the African field: hands-on, adapted to varying skill levels, and delivered by trainers with genuine mining experience.

02 — Supply

Equipment Supply

From mill liners to explosives, from industrial pumps to flotation reagents — MINEFECT supplies African mines with equipment and consumables selected for their robustness in extreme conditions.

Our global supplier network, combined with our command of African logistics, guarantees reliable deliveries to even the most remote sites.

Engineering & Technical Consulting
