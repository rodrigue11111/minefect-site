Our Mission

The reference partner
for African mines

Africa holds some of the world's most significant mineral resources. Exploiting these resources demands reliable equipment, trained teams, and logistics partners capable of delivering to the most remote sites — on time.

MINEFECT was built to meet this challenge. We combine the quality of world-class manufacturers with a deep understanding of African operational realities, so every mine can meet its production objectives.

01 — Training

Professional Training

Accidents and productivity losses often stem from inadequate training. MINEFECT provides targeted programmes to strengthen technical and operational skills on site — from equipment operation to preventive maintenance and HSE management.

Our training programmes are designed for the African field: hands-on, adapted to varying skill levels, and delivered by trainers with genuine mining experience.

Equipment Supply

From mill liners to explosives, from industrial pumps to flotation reagents — MINEFECT supplies African mines with equipment and consumables selected for their robustness in extreme conditions.

Our global supplier network, combined with our command of African logistics, guarantees reliable deliveries to even the most remote sites.
